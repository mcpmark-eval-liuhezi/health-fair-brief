## 1) Research Corner: Head-to-Head Trial (NCT05822830)

**Title:** A Phase 3b, Randomized Controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Tirzepatide Compared to Semaglutide in Adults Who Have Obesity or Overweight With Weight Related Comorbidities

| Field | Registry Value (as reported today, Nov 11, 2026) |
|---|---|
| **NCT Number** | NCT05822830 |
| **Recruitment Status** | **Completed** |
| **Sponsor** | **Eli Lilly and Company** |
| **Phase** | **Phase 3b** |
| **Conditions** | Obesity; Overweight (with weight-related comorbidities, without type 2 diabetes) |
| **Interventions** | Drug: Tirzepatide vs. Drug: Semaglutide |
| **Duration** | ~74 weeks |

> **Talking point for visitors:** This large head-to-head Phase 3b trial comparing tirzepatide (Mounjaro/Zepbound) vs. semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy) in adults with obesity/overweight and weight-related comorbidities is now **completed** — results are being reviewed and published. It's a great conversation starter on how GLP-1-era weight-loss medications are being compared head-to-head. Encourage interested visitors to ask the team about current enrollment options in our own metabolic health programs.

**Registry link:** https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05822830
