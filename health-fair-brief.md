## 🔬 Research Corner: The Head-to-Head Trial (NCT05822830)

**Title:** A Phase 3b, Randomized Controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Tirzepatide Compared to Semaglutide in Adults Who Have Obesity or Overweight With Weight Related Comorbidities

| Field | Registry value (as reported today) |
|---|---|
| **NCT Number** | NCT05822830 |
| **Recruitment Status** | **COMPLETED** (no longer enrolling) |
| **Sponsor** | **Eli Lilly and Company** |
| **Phase** | **Phase 3b** (interventional, randomized, ~74-week study; adults with obesity/overweight + weight-related comorbidities, *without* Type 2 diabetes) |
| **Conditions** | Obesity; Overweight |
| **Interventions** | Drug: Tirzepatide vs. Drug: Semaglutide |

**Talking point for visitors:** This landmark head-to-head trial has finished enrollment and follow-up, so it's now a completed study (results are being analyzed/published). It compared two injectable weight-management medications head-to-head in adults with obesity or overweight and weight-related comorbidities. If a visitor asks "Is there anything still enrolling on this exact study?" the accurate answer is **no** — but many *other* obesity and metabolic-health studies **are** actively recruiting right now (see list below; most are near-/far-site options, not Chicago-only).

**Registry link:** https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05822830
